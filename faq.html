Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common queries regarding ticketing, modifications, fees, and carrier policies.

How does Zippy Bus work?

Zippy Bus is an independent phone-based booking service. When you call +1-888-394-1623, you are connected with a travel agent who searches live bus carrier inventories, compares fares, books your tickets, and issues confirmations directly to you.

Is Zippy Bus affiliated with official bus carriers?

No. Zippy Bus is an independent travel referral and booking service. We are not officially affiliated with or endorsed by Greyhound, FlixBus, Megabus, Trailways, or any other official bus transit provider.

Are there service fees when booking by phone?

Yes. Tickets booked or modified through our agents may include a convenience service fee to cover our agent support and itinerary management services. Travelers always retain the choice to book tickets directly on carrier websites without paying agency fees.

How do I receive my bus tickets?

Once your reservation is finalized over the phone, your digital e-ticket and boarding QR code are instantly sent to your email address and via SMS text message.

Can I change or cancel my bus schedule?

Yes. Call our desk at +1-888-394-1623. Depending on the carrier and fare class chosen, our agents will process schedule modifications or explain applicable carrier credits and refund rules.
