Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 15, 2026

1. Standard Bus Carrier Tickets

As an independent booking assistance service, we adhere strictly to the ticketing rules and policies set forth by the official bus carriers (e.g., Greyhound, FlixBus, Barons Bus, etc.). Standard bus tickets are generally non-refundable for cash once the purchase is complete.

2. Travel Vouchers and Credits

If you need to cancel your trip, you may be eligible to receive a travel voucher or credit for future use, provided the cancellation is made prior to the scheduled departure time (typically 2 to 24 hours in advance, depending on the specific carrier's rules). Travel vouchers are subject to the carrier's terms of use and may incur a cancellation or exchange penalty fee deducted by the carrier.

3. No-Shows and Missed Departures

If you miss your scheduled bus or fail to cancel your ticket prior to the required cutoff time, your ticket will be voided. No refunds, vouchers, or travel credits will be issued for no-shows under any circumstances.

4. Agency Service Fees

Please note that any agency or service fees charged by Zippy Bus LLC for booking, changing, or assisting with your travel arrangements over the phone are strictly non-refundable. These fees compensate our independent agents for their time, expertise, and the assistance provided at the time of your call, regardless of whether you ultimately take the trip or cancel it.
