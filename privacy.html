Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Zippy Bus ("we", "us", or "our") values your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is collected, used, and protected when you access our site or utilize our phone reservation assistance services.

1. Information We Collect

When you call our reservation agents or submit inquiry forms, we may collect personal details such as your full name, phone number, email address, travel origin and destination, travel dates, and payment details necessary to complete carrier ticket issuance.

2. Use of Information

We use your information exclusively to search bus schedules, process bookings with transit providers, deliver itinerary confirmations, and assist with modifications or cancellations.

3. Third-Party Carriers

In order to book your seat, your traveler details are shared with the respective third-party bus carrier operating your selected route. We do not sell or rent your personal information to marketing brokers.

4. Data Security

We implement standard encryption and procedural safeguards to secure personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at support@zippybus.online or call (607) 952-7259.
