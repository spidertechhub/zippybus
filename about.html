About Zippy Bus

Delivering personalized, agent-assisted bus travel solutions across the United States and Canada.

Human Support in a Digital World

Zippy Bus was founded on a simple principle: booking a long-distance bus ticket should not require wrestling with confusing automated apps or waiting hours on hold with regional carriers.

We provide telephone-based travel booking and itinerary support. Our trained booking specialists assist you with schedule lookups, fare comparisons, last-minute seat changes, and cancellation management.

Fast Turnaround

No waiting in ticket lines. One phone call gets your seat reserved and confirmation sent directly to your phone or email.

Reliable Guidance

We clarify baggage rules, stopovers, station check-in protocols, and schedule flexibility before you commit.

Dedicated Assistance

If your schedule changes, our agents handle rebooking and modification requests on your behalf with ease.

Speak With a Live Travel Specialist

Ready to plan your trip? Call our reservation desk now for prompt routing and ticketing assistance.
